Objection overlooks requirement that failure probabilities be valid at **all four contexts**.

Write \(p_{ab}=f(a)+g(b)\). Additivity implies
\[
p_{01}+p_{10}=p_{00}+p_{11}=0.
\]
Both \(p_{01}\) and \(p_{10}\) must be nonnegative. Their sum is zero, so each equals zero. Shared conditional mechanism therefore gives deployment failure probability \(p_{01}=0\).

**Lab’s target is identified**, despite lack of separately varying features in source. Indeed, entire failure-probability surface is identified as zero.

Separate functions remain unidentified: for any constant \(c\),
\[
f(0)=f(1)=c,\qquad g(0)=g(1)=-c
\]
produces identical probabilities. No assumption requires individual contributions to be nonnegative.

Reviewer’s general concern about separating additive contributions is valid, but does not establish uncertainty in this particular target. Exact population zeros, additivity, and probability constraints jointly force it; observing zero failures in finite samples would not supply those exact equalities.
