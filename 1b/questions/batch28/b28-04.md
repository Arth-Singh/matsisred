## Prompt
A source evaluation contains binary contexts (A,B)=(0,0), (1,0), and (0,1), with exact binary failure probabilities 0.2, 0.4, and 0.5. Deployment consists of the unobserved source context (1,1). The same conditional mechanism applies in both populations. Independent engineering knowledge guarantees additive failure probabilities f(A)+g(B), valid at all four contexts, with no interaction.

A reviewer argues that absent source support at (1,1) makes deployment failure probability unidentified even with additivity and exact source means. Assess the inference and state what follows for deployment. At most 160 words.

## Rubric
The objection fails. Additivity yields mu11=.4+.5−.2=.7, a valid probability. Shared structure plus three independent cell contrasts identifies target even though ordinary importance weighting cannot.

## Justification
The issue is whether the actual information and structural restrictions determine the claimed target. Interior probabilities avoid accidental identification from boundary constraints. The boundary control separately tests that probability bounds can matter, and the no-additivity control prevents treating them as a universal extrapolation rule.

## Construction
