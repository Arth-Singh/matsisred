## Prompt
A source evaluation population has binary features A and B. Its only configurations are (0,0), (1,0), and (0,1), each with positive frequency. Unlimited labeled data establish conditional mean failure counts 0, 1, and 2 respectively. Deployment uses only (1,1), as unlimited unlabeled deployment data establish. Failure counts need not lie between zero and one.

Independent engineering knowledge guarantees that the conditional mean in both populations has the same additive form f(A)+g(B), with no interaction. The functions are unknown. The lab concludes deployment's mean is identified as 3.

A reviewer objects: “There are no labels at deployment's configuration. Reweighting source data cannot create that cell. Even granting additivity, deployment performance remains unidentified; this is a population-support problem, not a sample-size problem.”

Assess the conclusion, distinguishing failure of weighting from failure of identification. At most 170 words.

## Rubric
The objection fails. Weighting cannot create the target cell, but additivity gives mu11=mu10+mu01−mu00=3. The independently supplied structural restriction and three observed cells identify the missing mean.

## Justification
Zero target support prevents unrestricted nonparametric transport, not every extrapolation justified by supplied structure.
