Objection fails in supplied system. Delivered bit \(N\) is an actual causal mediator. Original bit sits upstream; its effect cannot bypass \(N\).

All front-door requirements hold:

- **Interception:** Every directed path from \(T\) to \(Y\) passes through \(N\).
- **No \(T\)–\(N\) confounding:** Independent channel noise leaves no open backdoor path from \(T\) to \(N\).
- **Blocked \(N\)–\(Y\) confounding:** Conditioning on \(T\) blocks backdoor path \(N \leftarrow \text{original bit} \leftarrow T \leftarrow U \rightarrow Y\).
- **Positivity:** Both training values occur, and each delivered bit has positive probability under either training value: \(1/4\) or \(3/4\).

Thus observational records identify
\[
p(y\mid do(T=t))
=\sum_n p(n\mid t)\sum_{t'}p(y\mid n,t')p(t').
\]

Randomization’s location matters: channel noise acts **before outcome computation**, changing its actual input. If noise affected only a recorded measurement while outcome computation still received original bit, observed \(N\) would merely be a proxy and would fail interception. Here hardware prevents that bypass. Training randomization is unnecessary for identification under stated assumptions.
