Objection fails under supplied causal model. Measured, pre-treatment \(C\) blocks sole backdoor path \(T\leftarrow C\rightarrow Y\). Complete graph and independent disturbances therefore justify adjustment:

\[
\mathbb E[Y\mid do(T=1)]-\mathbb E[Y\mid do(T=0)]
=
\sum_{c=0}^{1}P(C=c)
\left(\mathbb E[Y\mid T=1,C=c]-\mathbb E[Y\mid T=0,C=c]\right).
\]

Strictly positive joint probabilities ensure both treatment levels occur within each configuration, making every required conditional mean observable without extrapolation. Weighting by population frequencies of \(C\) targets population effect because assigning \(T\) leaves \(C\) unchanged.

Conditioning does not physically randomize treatment. Causal interpretation follows from supplied graph and disturbance assumptions, which establish conditional absence of confounding. Positivity supplies overlap; it does not remove confounding. Unlimited, error-free data identify required observational quantities, but do not alone make observational comparisons causal.
