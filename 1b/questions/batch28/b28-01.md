## Prompt
A lab wants to transfer a binary AI failure outcome Y from source to deployment. Two binary context features A and B are measured perfectly. The conditional failure probability given (A,B) is identical in source and deployment; there is no omitted feature, measurement error or concept shift. Unlimited labeled source data put half the mass on (0,0), where failure probability is 0.2, and half on (1,1), where it is 0.8. Deployment contains only (0,1), whose outcomes are unavailable.

The lab argues: “Both values of each feature occur in the source, and the failure mechanism is stable. Reweighting source records must therefore identify deployment's failure rate. Any remaining problem is estimation precision.”

Assess that argument. Give the missing information or a valid additional structural assumption that would resolve identification. Explain why any structural repair you propose is sufficient for this particular source design, not just a familiar way to handle covariate shift. At most 210 words.

## Rubric
Reject the inference: no joint source support for deployment. Additivity alone is not a sufficient repair: mu=.2+.6A versus mu=.2+.6B fit both source means and additivity but yield target .2/.8. Valid replies can propose labeled target outcomes, restored joint coverage, or an explicit sufficient restriction such as Y independent of B conditional on A, which gives .2 (or independence of A given B, giving .8). Merely saying justified structural extrapolation is needed is acceptable diagnosis but does not fulfill a claimed specific repair. Score primary verdict separately from any actual repair.

## Justification
The issue is whether the actual information and structural restrictions determine the claimed target. Interior probabilities avoid accidental identification from boundary constraints. The boundary control separately tests that probability bounds can matter, and the no-additivity control prevents treating them as a universal extrapolation rule.

## Construction
Manual five-item batch prompted by an audit caveat: additivity plus two zero means and nonnegative outcomes CAN identify the unobserved means. Do not count such a repair as universally wrong. Includes interior generation, explicit invalid repair, boundary positive, three-cell positive, and missing-structure negative controls.
