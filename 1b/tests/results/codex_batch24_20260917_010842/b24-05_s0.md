Selection probability is zero for M=1, so inverse-probability weighting of archived records alone cannot recover that stratum’s outcomes. However, verified apparatus property supplies its outcome mean exactly: 1. Census supplies each regime’s stratum proportions.

By partitioning over M,
\[
E[Y\mid R=r]
=P(M=0\mid R=r)E[Y\mid R=r,M=0]
+P(M=1\mid R=r)\cdot1.
\]
Thus,
\[
E[Y\mid R=0]=0.8(0.3)+0.2=0.44,
\]
\[
E[Y\mid R=1]=0.2(0.6)+0.8=0.92.
\]

Random assignment makes these source-population regime means causal. Total average effect of changing R from 0 to 1 is therefore
\[
\boxed{0.92-0.44=0.48}.
\]

Identification comes from combining archived conditional means, census proportions, and verified apparatus property—not weighting archived records alone.
