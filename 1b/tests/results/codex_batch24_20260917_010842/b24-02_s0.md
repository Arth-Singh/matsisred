Reply’s controlled-direct-effect conclusion follows under supplied model.

Write \(Y=f_Y(R,M,U_Y)\). Independent disturbances imply \(U_Y\) is independent of \((R,M)\), so selecting \(M=0\) does not select outcome disturbances. Hence
\[
E[Y\mid R=r,M=0]=E[Y(r,0)].
\]
Archive positivity makes both conditional means identifiable. Their difference therefore identifies source-population controlled direct effect
\[
E[Y(1,0)-Y(0,0)].
\]
Conditioning on this mediator blocks mediated path; it does not create outcome confounding under supplied assumptions. Reviewer’s collider-bias objection fails here.

Holding \(M=0\) by intervention differs from changing \(R\) and allowing \(M\) to respond naturally. Latter total effect is
\[
E[Y(1,M(1))-Y(0,M(0))],
\]
which archive generally cannot identify: it lacks source mediator frequencies and outcomes at \(M=1\). Archive contrast therefore supports joint interventions fixing \(M\), not an unrestricted regime-switch effect.
